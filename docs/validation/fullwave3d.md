The higher-frequency response is near a resonance: the fixed-radius mesh change is an
accuracy limit even if the radius-pair difference is smaller. The upper-frequency radius difference does not
decrease monotonically under this h refinement; ND1-to-ND2 improvement supports discretization as the cause,
but these runs do not establish 1e-4 absolute accuracy near resonance. Do not infer more digits from solver residuals.

Loop coarse-to-fine changes: max relative R 0.0137949, max relative L 0.00330823 over the five frequencies.
These mesh changes and solver residuals are distinct from the thin-wire reference-model uncertainty.
The strong decrease from ND1 to ND2 supports finite-element discretization as the remaining radius dependence;
omitting the exterior term leaves a much larger error. This is a convergence inference, not exact radius independence.
The ND1 comparison uses one rank and ND2 uses 16; solver residuals are shown separately.
Radius variation is a discretization/solver check; h/p comparisons above delimit the accuracy.
The slit-toroid comparison with arXiv:2211.00008 is qualitative: it differs from the paper’s geometry and feed.
Its 11 frequency points have maximum true residual 7.95354e-09.
This single-mesh sweep does not establish h/p convergence of its resonance.
Its recorded warnings are: 3 inverted curved elements were straightened locally (geometry approximated there); 3D full wave: the readout is capacitive (L(omega) <= 0) at some frequencies, i.e. above its first self-resonance; F and Q_rad are omitted there.
