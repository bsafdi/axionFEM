# Energy identity, field export and reactive-load closure (3D full wave)

!!! warning "Beta"
    These 3D full-wave diagnostics (energy matrices, field export, probes, source controls, validation loads) are
    beta.

The closed lossless problem can be solved with AMS-GMRES or with a reused STRUMPACK real-block factorization; see
[boundaries and solvers](fullwave_stage2a_boundaries_solver.md).
Each frequency assembles one Maxwell operator and reuses its preconditioner
or factor for every port and optional validation solve. The true-residual gate remains
`1e-6`; the requested iterative tolerance can be tightened with `AXF_FW_RTOL`.
`AXF_FW_MAX_ITER` changes the iteration ceiling (default 3000), while
`AXF_FW_KDIM` changes the restart dimension (default 600). All three settings are exported; none changes the
acceptance criteria.

## Energy and field normalization

For the lossless, nondispersive closed PEC problem, the solver retains the
complex electric field of every unit incoming port-current drive. It exports
the full Hermitian cross matrices, in joules per ampere squared:

```
energy_electric_J_per_A2 = E^dagger M E / 4
energy_magnetic_J_per_A2 = E^dagger K E / (4 omega^2)
energy_total_J_per_A2 = G
U = I^dagger G I
d Im Z / d omega = -4 G
```

The matrix fields use `{re: [[...]], im: [[...]]}`. The derivative output is
`d_ImZ_domega_from_energy_H`; its imaginary part should vanish for this
lossless reciprocal problem. The identity differentiates at fixed geometry
and fixed component settings. Differentiating a retuned sequence would not
test it. Material loss suppresses the derivative output; radiation boundaries
do not export this energy because their frequency-dependent exterior needs a
separate stored-energy treatment. Every row also carries the
exact `dZ_domega_H` from reciprocity, which holds with loss and with the
radiating exterior and gives the full complex derivative; in the closed lossless
case its imaginary part matches this identity to ~1e-11
(`dZ_domega_vs_energy_identity_relative`; [multiport_fullwave.md](../physics/multiport_fullwave.md)). Omitted capacitor, ESL, line and input-coil
energy must be added before calibrating a fixed loaded-mode Q. The legacy
scalar `L_H`, `F_SI_T4_H2_m8` and `Q_bulk` fields do not perform that calibration.

`fullwave.field_export_directory` saves each rank's local MFEM mesh and every
frequency/port real and imaginary GridFunction, with a manifest. No image
factor is applied. `fullwave.field_currents={re:[...],im:[...]}` combines the
unit-port fields at every frequency and exports `combined` fields; at the
scene frequency this combination becomes the scene field. It includes only
the fields generated by the supplied incoming port currents. An independent
impressed-source field is not silently included. Direct source and directly
loaded validation fields are exported when those checks are enabled.

Saved serial meshes must be loaded with refinement marking disabled, for
example `mfem::Mesh(stream, 1, 0, false)`. The third argument is `refine`;
`refine=1` can reorder tetrahedron vertices and edges without permuting the
subsequently loaded GridFunction coefficients. `fix_orientation=false` alone
does not prevent it. Loaded this way, the saved fields reproduce the native
E/B probes and the solver's own source overlaps.

`fullwave.field_probes=[[x,y,z],...]` exports every port's complex E and B,
with `B=curl E/(i omega)`. The MPI mesh search assigns exactly one owning
rank; physical RF-volume validity is exported separately. A point outside
the mesh, in retained PEC or in the mapped shell has a zero sample and a zero
validity flag. Such a sample must not be used as a physical zero. The probes
support explicit vacuum-gap comparisons and magnetic-flux quadrature for
residual-return loops; they do not establish flux-quadrature convergence.

`fullwave.source_controls=[{name,source,quadrature_order},...]` assembles extra
source overlap vectors on the same RF mesh and reuses all retained field
columns. `source` uses the normal prescribed-source schema, including file
references; `quadrature_order=0` selects MFEM's default rule. Unique names label
`source_control_Psi_vectors` in each frequency row. Each assembly is timed
separately in the top-level `source_controls` list. Separate controls can
change only the filament arc/pack quadrature or only the FE integration rule.
They add no Maxwell RHS but can still cost substantial source-evaluation time.
Transparent RF coil regions are included in these overlaps; their vacuum volume
is not suppressed implicitly.

## Direct reactive closure

`fullwave.validation_loads` is an array with one object per port. Each object
defines a series branch `{R_ohm, L_H, C_F}`, with absent values zero and an
all-zero object denoting an open port. These loads are exclusive of the old
`validation_shunt_R_ohm` list. For `exp(-i omega t)`:

```
Z_load = R - i omega L + i/(omega C)   (omit C term when C=0)
A_loaded = A - i omega B Y B^T
C-only update = -omega^2 B C B^T
L-only update = +B L^-1 B^T
V = (1 + Z Y)^-1 Z e_0
I_in = e_0 - Y V
E_loaded = E I_in
```

The direct loaded FEM voltage is compared with the circuit solution for
every component, including a weak readout. Each port must satisfy a relative
tolerance of `1e-5` plus an explicit absolute tolerance of `1e-12` times the
largest predicted voltage. The whole-vector error is also exported. The
direct loaded field must agree with the port-field reconstruction to `1e-5`.
Both fields are saved when field export is enabled. An ideal branch exactly
at its series LC singularity is rejected; finite series resistance is needed
there. The direct-source reciprocity check uses the same componentwise gate.

Failed port, direct-source, direct-load and observable gates retain a
`provisional_rejected` diagnostic on rank zero before raising the original
error. With field export it is `f_{frequency_index}_rejected.json` in that
directory; otherwise it is `fullwave_rejected.json` in the solve workdir.
The artifact contains the partial model result, accepted earlier frequencies,
the rejected `primary_row`, all available Z/Psi/G/source-control overlaps,
field-file references, timing and true/preconditioned residual diagnostics.
Missing unsolved matrix columns are JSON null, not physical zeros. Rejected
fields and numbers must not be used as accepted sweep values. Diagnostic I/O
failure falls back to a rank-zero stderr payload.

Direct-source diagnostics preserve the weak terminal's cancellation scales
and the exact discrete transpose identity
`V_direct-V_recip=(s^T r_port-E_port^T r_source)/(i omega)`, with
`r=A E-rhs` and unit-current port fields. Its two contributions and numerical
remainder distinguish port-solve and source-solve error; they do not waive
either gate. The absolute true-DOF sums depend on the finite-element basis
and are diagnostic bounds, not observable error estimates. A small-matrix
complex-symmetric check verifies the identity and that an inexact solution
still fails the unchanged componentwise criterion.

## Tests

`tests/test_fullwave_energy_reference.py` checks the algebra on small matrices: the fixed-geometry reactance slope
against the total-energy cross matrix, the capacitor/inductor/ESL updates and loaded field with a deliberately weak
readout, the componentwise visibility of a weak-readout error, and the source-reciprocity transpose identity for an
inexact solution. These are algebra checks, not FEM qualification. The FEM-level derivative is checked against a
centred difference in `tests/test_fullwave3d_dzdw_terminations.py`, the AMS and STRUMPACK paths against an exact TEM
line in `tests/test_fullwave3d_solver_paths.py`, and the parser guards (control names, quadrature order, loads, field
currents) in `tests/test_multiport_fem_spec.py`. None of these establishes a detector's physical pole, source
convergence, spatial convergence or tuned-mode Q.

Reports include port-source and magnetic source assembly, energy-operator setup, per-frequency solver setup, each
right-hand side, energy post-processing, total frequency wall time and maximum/summed per-rank peak RSS. Meshing,
other model setup and process startup are not included.
